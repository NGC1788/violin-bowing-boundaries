# Learned selection under a fixed law decision certificate

Separate campaign. Budget is one fixed HGB fit, three selection policies and one replay per policy and case, with a 600-second wall limit. Reuse the existing HGB configuration with seed 0 and train on all A/B source columns only. Evaluation uses all 12 C/D folders and tolerance ladder 0.2, 0.4, 0.8 and 1.6 at slope −2. The ladder is fixed before computing outcomes to exercise real compatible cases under wider tolerances. Report each tolerance and both outcomes. Repeated tolerances on a folder are dependent and do not increase the independent sample count. The historical data are exposed. Eligibility, nine rules and the stopping certificate are inherited. Public measured force, beta, speed and rule thresholds may reach the prior. Evaluation labels can only reach the acquisition state through queries. Independent full-data scoring checks the terminal decision.

Policies use the same live rules, binding endpoint weights and tie break as law_query_policy_v1. Each candidate first-run start votes for observing its two required positive bits and its required negative predecessor. Weight a vote by the predicted chance the query contradicts the corresponding necessary bit. Uniform 0.5 recovers the previous law decision priority exactly, since all votes are multiplied by 0.5. The second control uses A/B per-rule prevalence with the existing pseudocount. The third uses the fixed HGB conditional on public features. The score counts expected eliminations due to these local necessary conditions. It omits other ways of eliminating starts and does not model joint rule probabilities. Marginals suffice for the expected sum of votes. The certificate remains independent of prior quality.

Record all query counts, correctness, fit time and acquisition runtime. Mark HGB as beneficial only if total queries are strictly lower than both controls over all cases and separately in both outcome classes. Report regressions and the original 0.2 results even if a wider tolerance benefits. Include zero-query cases in totals. No tuning, additional seeds or retries based on scores.
