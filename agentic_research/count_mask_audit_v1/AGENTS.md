# Synthetic count-mask inference audit

- Main research is AI/information: learned forecasting and efficient differentiable inference. Violin is an application dataset, not a new physical-law claim.
- This is a separate one-attempt numerical audit. No actual data/NPZ/raw/labels/predictions/weights may be accessed or hashed. No model training or data/model download.
- Bind code, fixtures, criteria, dependency sources, review and written prior review before running. Preserve existing campaigns and their failures unchanged.
- Test exact enumeration, count DP, guard/residual, local decoder gradients and fixed-law coarsening. Do not generalize conditional iid model results to arbitrary observer errors, MNAR or correlated Gaussian innovations.
- Keep absolute beam probabilities. Surviving state mass is not full-state probability. Report actual residual; beam sizes need not be nested.
- Consume the remaining shared numerical audit attempt atomically before evaluation, within600seconds/deadline. Failure consumes it too. No refreeze/retry after start.
- Separate mathematical truncation bounds from floating-point and statistical guarantees. Passing is not evidence of novelty or forecasting improvement.
- Retain important numerical intermediates and timings. Public outputs are toy-only aggregates and source hashes; private locks/start/failure records remain local.
