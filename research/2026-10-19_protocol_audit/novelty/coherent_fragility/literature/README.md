# Coherent threshold perturbations: prior art and interpretation

2026-10-21. Reviewer: Codex `coherent_theory`. This is a bounded primary-source review and a methodological audit prepared before inspecting this phase's empirical output. It does not certify an implementation or assert an empirical success. Shared paper, raw data and running pipelines were not changed.

## Assessment

Replacing independent label edits with one waveform-only rule shared by every observation is a scientifically useful restriction. It asks whether a worst-case combinatorial witness is realizable by a declared classifier family. It also exposes the labels changed elsewhere by the same rule. **The new object is exact, rule-constrained sensitivity of a discontinuous boundary-to-law pipeline; threshold sweeping itself is established.**

The strongest result would distinguish three nested obstacles: finite force-grid support, label configurations allowed by a single coherent classifier, and simultaneous compatibility across measured folders. A negative result can be useful: if arbitrary edits restore compatibility but every allowed common threshold fails, small unrestricted Hamming costs do not establish a plausible classifier explanation. Such a result is conditional on the examined one-parameter families, not all possible coherent classifiers.

## Closest primary sources

1. **Threshold sweeps and score ties.** Tom Fawcett, “An introduction to ROC analysis,” *Pattern Recognition Letters* 27 (2006), 861–874, DOI [10.1016/j.patrec.2005.10.010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patrec.2005.10.010). [Full article](https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~saito/data/roc/fawcett-roc.pdf), §5, Algorithm 1. Sorting scores and sweeping threshold-induced classification states is standard; tied scores must be handled together. That article concerns predictive performance, while our downstream target is a force-law compatibility predicate. Neither exact enumeration of unique threshold states nor sorting-based efficiency is a new general algorithm here.

2. **Preprocessing alternatives and scientific conclusions.** Sara Steegen, Francis Tuerlinckx, Andrew Gelman and Wolf Vanpaemel, “Increasing Transparency Through a Multiverse Analysis,” *Perspectives on Psychological Science* 11(5) (2016), 702–712, DOI [10.1177/1745691616658637](https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691616658637). [Author-hosted full article](https://stat.columbia.edu/~gelman/research/published/multiverse_published.pdf). The paper explicitly includes alternative split points, coding and exclusions and recommends exposing the dependence of results on reasonable processing choices. Therefore “classifier thresholds can affect scientific conclusions” is not new. Our distinction is complete event-level coverage for a specified scalar family, together with a minimum-change witness and explicit support restrictions.

3. **Specification choice is not sampling uncertainty.** Uri Simonsohn, Joseph P. Simmons and Leif D. Nelson, “Specification curve analysis,” *Nature Human Behaviour* 4 (2020), 1208–1214, DOI [10.1038/s41562-020-0912-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-020-0912-z). [Author-hosted full article](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/33-Simonsohn-Simmons-Nelson-2020.pdf). The authors separate defensible specifications from motivated result selection and display alternatives transparently. Our deterministic frontier does not perform their joint statistical inference, nor does enumerating every chosen threshold establish that all thresholds are scientifically defensible.

4. **Rashomon sets need an accuracy restriction.** Charles Marx, Flavio Calmon and Berk Ustun, “Predictive Multiplicity in Classification,” ICML 2020, PMLR 119:6765–6774. [Official paper and PDF](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/marx20a.html), §2, Definitions 1–2. Their competing classifiers lie in a near-optimal predictive-error set. Our waveform thresholds have no independent physical truth labels or comparable performance guarantee. They form a declared sensitivity family, not an accuracy-certified Rashomon set. Agreement with the original classifier is a perturbation cost, not predictive accuracy.

5. **Restricted fragility is already a concept.** Benjamin R. Baer et al., “Fragility indices for only sufficiently likely modifications,” *PNAS* 118(49) (2021), e2105254118, DOI [10.1073/pnas.2105254118](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2105254118). Its exact and plausibility-restricted fragility concepts were reviewed in [the preceding phase](../../run_fragility/literature/README.md). Coherence is a specific admissibility restriction; it should not be advertised as the first restricted fragility index. No fresh full-text review of that article was performed this phase.

6. **The acoustics motivation is already partly known.** Homayoon Beigi and Grace Conneely, [arXiv:2609.14990v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14990v3), §II-H, already discuss multiple H/non-H force crossings and time-window dependence of measured exponent summaries. The present extension concerns exact shared waveform-rule attainability, not the discovery that boundary analyses depend on processing. The descriptive slope −1.2 is our comparison value; it is not the paper's exact proposed β exponent −1.

No matching exact combination of a common waveform threshold, first-K-run force boundaries, profiled-law compatibility and all-observation collateral edits was located in this bounded review. That is not a claim of worldwide priority.

## What can be certified exactly

Let the fixed waveform features of observation i be z_i and let h_t(z_i) be a one-parameter rule. Other gates and upstream feature extraction are fixed. The same numeric t is used for all 78,000 observations, with no measured force, β, folder, session identifier or target law used as an input to h_t. Choosing t by a law objective is an audit search, not an improved physical classifier.

A scalar comparison changes truth value only at observed feature scores, fixed gate values and the declared parameter-domain endpoints. On each resulting parameter cell, the complete label vector is constant; therefore every deterministic downstream boundary, admissibility flag, compatibility verdict and Hamming count is also constant. Evaluating every realized label state certifies the optimum over the declared family. A coarse grid of thresholds would not provide that guarantee.

For `score >= t`, labels at an observed score belong to the lower-threshold side; for `score < t`, they belong to the higher-threshold side. These are different closure conventions. Tied scores change as one indivisible group. Domains clipped at observed scores can create endpoint-only states. Store exact endpoint closure, a finite representative satisfying the interval, and the direct-evaluation witness. Never split equal scores to create a label assignment that no common threshold can realize.

Monotone individual labels do **not** imply monotone law compatibility. Tightening a positive acceptance gate may make each first run move only upward or disappear, yet different β columns move at different threshold events. The largest lower intercept and smallest upper intercept can exchange identities, so the compatibility set can have several components. A binary search for the first successful threshold is not generally sufficient.

For folder f and slope p, define `S_f(p)` as the thresholds that leave every originally eligible column with a finite positive first-run bracket and make the common-intercept intersection nonempty. This is a union of parameter cells. A folder-specific best t is an **oracle**. One rule works on a collection Q of folders only if `intersection_{f in Q} S_f(p)` is nonempty. The union of individually attainable folders is only an upper bound on simultaneous coverage.

At a threshold t define full-data disagreement `G(t) = sum over all 78,000 i of 1[h_t(z_i) != h_base(z_i)]`, eligible-window disagreement and affected-column count separately. The minimum of G over S_f is a globally applied rule selected to rescue one folder, not a simultaneous rescue of all folders. A coverage–collateral frontier reporting `(G(t), number of compatible folders, number of inadmissible folders)` gives the practical consequences without hiding changes elsewhere. Include the identities of covered folders, since different states can have equal counts but different membership.

The preceding unrestricted minimum cost is a lower bound on a coherent rule's **within-folder eligible-window** edit count whenever both use the same eligible columns, finite-state restrictions, p and ε. It is also a lower bound on G, but comparing only G against that local lower bound confounds coherence within the target folder with collateral changes elsewhere. Report local excess and external changes separately. An infinite unrestricted cost caused by unsupported force geometry remains impossible for every coherent rule under the same support convention.

## Analysis requirements

- **Freeze eligibility.** Retain the original K=2 finite-positive boundary columns. A threshold that removes an original boundary, makes it left-censored or creates a nonpositive/zero-width bracket fails the admissibility condition for that folder. It must not count as compatibility by silently dropping the column. Record failure reasons and counts.
- **Keep the target explicit.** The all-column ±0.2 log-force tube is an operational predicate, not the old MLE confidence interval. ε is neither a sensor uncertainty calibration nor a confidence level. The classifier threshold and law-tolerance ε are distinct parameters.
- **Main scope is one varied gate at a time.** Periodicity [0.7,0.9], second-flyback ratio [0.3,0.7], and additional amplitude/flyback-height gates are separate families. This does not search their two- or four-dimensional combinations or every physically sensible classifier. Do not infer a certificate over unsearched combinations.
- **Distinguish plausibility levels.** Local periodicity/ratio ranges are declared perturbations, not validated equally accurate classifiers. Full [0,1] ranges are stress tests. Absolute amplitude/flyback-height gates across strings and sessions can reflect sensor gain, signal level or string-specific amplitude; success there does not establish a physically correct regime boundary.
- **Hold feature extraction fixed.** Varying a final ratio gate is not the same as recomputing detected peak counts or waveform segmentation under a new extraction algorithm. State exactly which quantity varies and which stored features remain fixed.
- **Don't optimize toward the favored law and then call it confirmation.** A target-law-selected t supplies an attainable counterfactual analysis. Its physical correctness would require independent labels or independent data not used for the selection. All current data are already seen.
- **Count what is actually shared.** The threshold must be one absolute value across all folders. Per-folder thresholds, quantiles recomputed per folder, hidden session-specific normalizations, or feature gates using F/β would solve easier and different questions.
- **Check intervals, not just a lucky point.** Report whether successful threshold components have positive width or exist only at ties/domain endpoints. The width is numerical stability within this family, not a probability of correctness. Different feature scales prevent comparing raw widths across families.
- **Preserve negative cases.** A no-success result across every event cell is a certificate within the declared family. It is not a failure to search hard enough and should not trigger an unreported expansion of thresholds or a force-conditioned rule. Expanded families need a separate, clearly post hoc declaration.
- **Avoid probabilities from threshold fractions.** The proportion of grid cells or threshold length yielding a verdict depends on parameterization and score density. It is not a posterior probability or a repeated-sampling confidence statement. The 39 folders are also not 39 independent experiments.

## What counts as a meaningful outcome

1. **A local-range threshold restores one or more folders:** show the entire success interval, all-data collateral edits, and exact within-folder cost gap versus arbitrary edits. This establishes an explicit consistent rule that changes the operational verdict; it does not select a true law.
2. **Only stress-test or uncalibrated amplitude gates succeed:** report limited attainability and the associated costs/boundary losses. This weakens the interpretation that ordinary plausible tuning explains the original result.
3. **Each folder can be rescued only with conflicting thresholds:** demonstrate the distinction between per-folder oracle feasibility and a common global rule. This is more informative than listing each best result independently.
4. **No declared threshold succeeds although arbitrary labels do:** report a coherence obstruction. The unrestricted certificate remains mathematically correct but is too permissive to support a classifier-based explanation within these families.
5. **Maximum common coverage is small:** preserve the residual counterexamples and identify whether they arise from geometry, classifier-family restrictions or invalidated boundaries. Do not summarize only the successful subset.

## Defensible contribution wording

“We compare unrestricted classification fragility with exact sensitivity to a shared waveform decision rule. By enumerating every realized scalar-threshold state, we determine which first-run boundary-law compatibility reversals are attainable without observation-specific relabeling, and quantify the collateral changes and boundary losses across all measured conditions. This separates sparse adversarial label edits from coherent classifier-induced changes.”

The general sweep, multiverse rationale and restricted-fragility idea are established. The contribution candidate is their problem-specific integration, independent verification and a scientifically consequential difference between arbitrary and realizable perturbations. It is a focused computational acoustics/audit contribution, not yet a new physical force law, a calibrated classifier, or evidence of top-tier general machine-learning algorithmic novelty.
