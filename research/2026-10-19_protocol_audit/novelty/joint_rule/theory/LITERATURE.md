# Prior art and contribution boundaries for joint-rule certificates

Primary sources inspected 2026-10-21. This is a targeted check, not evidence that no closely related work exists. No empirical joint-rule result was used to select the methodological claim below.

## Assessment

The two-threshold extension by itself is incremental sensitivity analysis. The useful technical unit is the **end-to-end, independently checkable feasible set**, including an explicit distinction between (i) force-grid feasibility, (ii) rule-reachable boundaries, (iii) shared-rule consistency across columns/folders and (iv) collateral loss of finite boundaries. Existing generic methods supply most algorithmic ingredients.

A defensible contribution statement is: “We provide exact shared-rule feasibility and obstruction certificates for run-defined empirical boundaries, preventing arbitrary label edits or separately optimized measurement conditions from being interpreted as an implementable common classification rule.” It remains a contribution candidate until the empirical results demonstrate a substantive distinction and independent verification checks the entire claimed region.

Do not use the number of cells evaluated as scientific sample size; do not interpret area fractions as probability of a conclusion without a distribution over thresholds; do not label a selected compatible rule ‘corrected’; do not turn an all-column operational decision into a rejection/acceptance claim for a universal physical law. The measured dataset is cello, not a violin validation dataset.

## Concrete next useful outputs

1. Exact feasible sets and common-rule intersections, including the baseline scalar slices.
2. For each impossible folder, a complete region cover with invalid-column or separating-pair witnesses. This explains whether failure is caused by measurement identifiability or mutually incompatible boundary columns.
3. A collateral-impact frontier with boundary loss separated from changed labels. Equal counts can hide which folders were lost; retain IDs.
4. Any new interior-only witness, if observed, reported as a failure of one-at-a-time robustness to cover joint changes. This phenomenon is generic; its measured magnitude and downstream consequence are the substantive result.

Adding calibrated limits on permissible changes would strengthen scientific credibility, but these data do not provide independent ground truth to justify such calibration. A future blinded expert-label subset or independently collected dataset would be needed for that claim; do not simulate one by agreement with the existing classifier.
