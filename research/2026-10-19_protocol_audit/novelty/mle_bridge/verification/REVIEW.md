# Independent interpretation review

**Verdict:** the minimum geometric obstructions are meaningful as a reproducible
starting point for likelihood-influence diagnostics, but they do not certify
which observations determine the original Gaussian MLE conclusion.

The most useful result is a verified failure of that implication. Gaussian
dispersion permits nonempty intervals that cannot all intersect a fixed-
tolerance law. Full likelihood inference also includes censoring and all three
speeds. Deleting a geometric obstruction can move the fitted beta slope away
from its target and can increase the fixed-versus-free likelihood contrast.
Those observations are compatible with both estimands; they are not a bug.

The counterexample is not confined to a lexicographically chosen certificate:
all 19 minimum triples in A1 v3 at target -1.2 and all five minimum triples in
B_T2r1 v1 at target -2 have that adverse direction for both reported metrics.
All 24 memberships and metrics were independently checked. The family search
is explicitly post hoc; selecting its best member cannot be turned into a
confirmation p-value.

The fixed-scale separated-pair bound is a valid deterministic likelihood
burden bound. It needs the original unexpanded interval gap, common covariate
offset, positive specified scale, and numerical separation rather than only
invalid-boundary evidence. It is not a likelihood-ratio lower bound and gives
no rejection after profiling unrestricted scale. Existing convexity results
support the independent canonical optimization but are not new methodology.

The contribution worth keeping is the explicit, reproducible bridge between
waveform-rule certificates, finite-interval geometry, censoring-aware MLE,
case-deletion influence, and full certificate-family selection. It prevents
a deterministic explanation from being misreported as statistical evidence.
It does not establish classifier accuracy, a new physical law, population
generalization, or violin measurements. Bootstrap uncertainty and the earlier
frozen results have not been replaced.
