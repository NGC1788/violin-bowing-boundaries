Checked 2026-10-21. These sources limit the novelty claim. They do not establish empirical correctness of this application. No result from a source is presented as applying automatically to censored bowed-string boundaries.

## Claim boundary

The plausible application-specific contribution is to test whether a small subset that certifies incompatibility uniformly over a shared waveform-rule domain also explains the existing likelihood result, using retained censoring, actual grouped refits, complete small-subset references, and a direct-versus-refit likelihood decomposition. The connection between rule geometry and measurement-law inference is the research object. Data deletion, influence, minimal inconsistent sets, profile likelihood and confidence-set fragility already have substantial literature.

No search here establishes that the full combination is the world's first. A null association between geometric witnesses and MLE influence would restrict the method to explaining the operational all-column predicate; it should be reported rather than replaced with a favorable diagnostic after looking at results. Stronger claims about valid selected-subset testing or statistical certificates would require a new selection-aware theory and appropriate dependence calibration.
